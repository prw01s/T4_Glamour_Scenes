Calibrate a room

Turns a day photo, a night photo and a wall mask into a room for the sheen visualizer: the camera and the walls (from straight lines in the photo) and the light sources. Detection proposes; you check the room grid and correct. Wheel to zoom, drag to pan (or right-drag / Space-drag in other modes), Esc to cancel a tool.

1. Photos

Same camera, same framing; the mask is black where the wall is (white elsewhere). Non-square images are cropped to their centre square.

2. Camera lines

The camera comes from lines that are straight in the room: verticals (corners, door and window sides) and two families of horizontals at right angles, a and b (frames, baseboards, sills, ceiling edges, tiles). The photo must have upright verticals (perspective-corrected).

ab verticalb sloped unused

Click a line to cycle its type (a → b → vertical → unused), or hover it and press A B V S (sloped) X (unused) Del. Drawn lines snap to the edge under them.

"b sloped": lines in two walls of direction b that are parallel but not horizontal (a sloped ceiling's edges), when the room shows no horizontal b lines.

3. Walls, floor, ceiling

Add the walls you see. The first is anchored anywhere on it; each next one at its corner with a wall already placed. Then click where a wall meets the floor (a baseboard's foot). Check the yellow grid: its lines must run along each wall and stop at the corners.

4. Light sources

Per photo (the view tab above chooses which): emitters are regions whose brightness the camera compressed (window glass, bulbs, lit shades), restored by a gain for the reflections. Lamps that stand off the walls are also placed in 3-D. Defaults: windows ×6, bulbs ×12, shades ×5.

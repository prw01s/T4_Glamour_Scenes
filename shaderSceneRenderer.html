This demo proves out two important milestones on the path to supporting rich Glamour Scene lighting variations with a performant and scalable approach.

The scene tinting uses a client-side, C-like shading language (GLSL) that maximizes the device’s GPU. No GenAI is used, and there is absolutely no cost associated with this tinting method.

The implementation gives us precise control over how paint colors are blended with the inherent color lighting within the room scene, including the ability to use algorithms that reflect the actual science of human color perception. As an example of the sophisticated control we have over the tinting logic, the “Tinting algo” control shows the results of subtly changing that logic.
